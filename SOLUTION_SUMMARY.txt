HOW TO USE (FOR USERS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Step 1: Check your setup
  $ python3 verify_setup.py
  
  This will tell you exactly what's missing or misconfigured.

Step 2: Install dependencies (if needed)
  $ pip install -e .
  
  This installs all required packages (torch, fairscale, fire, etc.)

Step 3: Download model (if needed)
  $ ./download.sh
  
  Follow the prompts to download the Llama 3 model (~15GB)

Step 4: Launch an app
  $ ./launch_app.sh
  
  Choose from 5 different apps and launch with guided setup
